## 1. Purpose

Provider agrees to design, build, deploy, and maintain a custom marketing website for Client's mobile detailing business, and Client agrees to pay the fees described in this Agreement.

---

## 2. Scope of Work — One-Time Website Project

Provider will deliver a single-page, mobile-responsive marketing website for **Old City Detail**, including:

### 2.1 Design & Development
- Custom homepage with sections for hero, services, pricing, about, gallery, reviews/testimonials, and contact
- Responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Sticky header with click-to-call phone call-to-action
- Mobile navigation menu
- Interactive pricing modals for service categories (auto, boat, RV, golf cart, airplane, and maintenance plans, as applicable)
- Testimonial carousel
- Smooth in-page navigation
- Basic on-page SEO (page title, meta description, LocalBusiness structured data)

### 2.2 Content & Deployment
- Implementation of Client-provided business information (name, phone, email, hours, service descriptions, and pricing)
- Deployment to a staging/preview URL for Client review
- Connection of Client's custom domain (e.g., oldcitydetailing.com) upon Client approval and once domain access is provided
- Brief handoff explaining how routine content updates may be requested

### 2.3 Not Included (Unless Added by Written Change Order)
- Logo design or full brand identity
- Professional copywriting beyond formatting Client-supplied content
- Professional photography or videography (Client may supply assets; Provider may use placeholders until Client assets are delivered)
- Paid advertising, social media management, or Google Business Profile optimization
- Online booking, payment processing, contact forms with backend/database, e-commerce, or customer login areas
- Additional pages beyond the agreed single-page site
- Email hosting or domain registration fees (Client pays registrar/third-party costs directly unless otherwise agreed)

---

## 3. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

1. Provide accurate business information, pricing, service lists, testimonials, and brand assets (logo, photos) in a timely manner.
2. Designate one primary point of contact for feedback and approvals.
3. Review preview links and provide consolidated feedback within **5 business days** of each review request.
4. Purchase and maintain domain registration and grant Provider access needed for DNS/domain connection.
5. Ensure Client has rights to all content (text, images, logos, reviews) supplied to Provider.

Delays caused by Client's failure to provide materials or feedback may extend the project timeline and are not Provider's responsibility.

---

## 4. Project Timeline

Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to complete the initial website build within **4–6 weeks** from the later of: (a) Effective Date, or (b) receipt of deposit and essential Client materials.

Launch to Client's live domain depends on Client approval and timely domain/DNS access.

---

## 5. Revisions

The one-time project fee includes **one (1) round of revisions** after Client reviews the preview site. Revisions are limited to reasonable adjustments within the agreed scope (copy tweaks, layout refinements, color/spacing adjustments).

Additional revision rounds or new features outside scope will be billed at **$85/hour** (or quoted separately in writing) and require Client approval before work begins.

## 7. Monthly Website Care Plan

Upon launch (or upon Client's request if launch is delayed by Client), Client may enroll in Provider's monthly Website Care Plan:

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| **Monthly Care Plan** | **$99.00 USD per month** |

### 7.1 Minimum Term
The monthly plan requires a **12-month initial term** beginning on the first billing date after site launch ("Care Plan Start Date"). After the initial term, the plan continues month-to-month unless cancelled under Section 7.4.

### 7.2 What's Included Each Month
- Hosting/deployment management on Provider's configured platform (e.g., Cloudflare Pages or equivalent)
- SSL/HTTPS monitoring via hosting platform
- Routine dependency and build updates as Provider deems necessary for security and stability
- Up to **30 minutes** of content updates per month, such as:
  - Price changes
  - Hours or contact info updates
  - Minor text edits
  - Replacing gallery images Client provides
  - Updating testimonial entries
- Email support for site-related issues (response within **2 business days**)

Unused monthly time does not roll over.

### 7.3 Not Included in Monthly Plan
- New pages, new features, redesigns, or new pricing modal structures
- Work exceeding 30 minutes/month (billed at **$85/hour** in 15-minute increments with Client approval)
- Third-party fees (domain renewal, paid stock media, paid plugins/services)
- Marketing, SEO campaigns, ads, or reputation management

### 7.4 Cancellation
After the 12-month initial term, either Party may cancel the monthly plan with **30 days' written notice**. Client remains responsible for monthly fees through the notice period.

**Early cancellation:** If Client cancels the monthly plan before completing the 12-month initial term, Client agrees to pay the remaining monthly fees for the initial term, **or** a **$300 early termination fee**, whichever is **less**.

### 7.5 Monthly Billing
Monthly fees are billed automatically on the same day each month (or invoiced net-7, at Provider's option) via [Stripe / invoice / payment method]. Client authorizes recurring charges for the monthly plan.

## 8. Optional: Client Declines Monthly Plan

- Provider is not obligated to provide ongoing updates, monitoring, or support after handoff.
- A one-time **Handoff & Training Fee** of **$250** may apply if Client requests managed handoff to another host or developer.
